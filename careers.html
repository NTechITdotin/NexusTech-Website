About NOBTECH IT Services

NOBTECH IT Services Pvt Ltd. is a Baramati-based product engineering company building SaaS platforms and client-facing web applications across healthcare, hospitality, and education. Our live portfolio includes DentaFlow, GymFlow, a custom Hotel Management System for Hotel Maitreya, and BCA Review Tracker — all built and deployed end-to-end by our in-house engineering team.

About the Internship

You'll contribute to real modules across our active platforms under the guidance of the founding engineering team, with exposure to the full lifecycle: architecture, development, deployment, and client delivery.

What You'll Work On

  • Feature development on live multi-tenant SaaS products (Angular 17+ with Signals, Node.js/Express, PostgreSQL)
  • Building and integrating REST APIs, role-based access control (RBAC), and billing/payments workflows
  • WhatsApp Business API integrations — automated notifications, reminders, and admin messaging tools
  • Agentic AI / LLM-powered features — prompt-driven workflows, AI-assisted admin tools, and automation agents
  • Cloud deployment and DevOps — Nginx, PM2, Jenkins CI/CD, AWS (EC2, RDS, S3), and production server administration
  • Writing and maintaining PRDs, technical specs, and documentation alongside the product engineering team

Required Skills

  • Solid fundamentals in JavaScript/TypeScript and at least one modern frontend framework (Angular or React)
  • Working knowledge of Node.js and REST API design
  • Understanding of relational databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL) — schema design, queries, migrations
  • Familiarity with Git/GitHub workflows
  • Willingness to work on-site in Baramati and learn production deployment practices hands-on

Good to Have

  • Exposure to Python and interest in Agentic AI / LLM application development (LangChain, OpenAI/Anthropic APIs, prompt engineering)
  • Basic understanding of AWS services (EC2, RDS, S3, Lambda)
  • Familiarity with CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitHub Actions) and process managers (PM2)
  • Prior personal projects, hackathon experience, or open-source contributions
  • Exposure to WhatsApp Business API, payment gateways (Razorpay/Stripe), or multi-tenant SaaS architecture

What We Offer

  • Direct mentorship from the founding engineering team — real production ownership from day one
  • Hands-on experience across the full SaaS lifecycle
  • Exposure to modern stacks and emerging areas like Agentic AI
  • Certificate of internship and potential for a full-time offer based on performance
